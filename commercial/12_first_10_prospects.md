# FIRST 10 TARGET PROSPECTS — US B2B IT & DEFENSE CONTRACTORS

**Document ID:** `COMM-DOC-012`  
**Reference:** M6 Commercial Activation  
**Target Profile:** US Small-to-Midsize MSPs, MSSPs, and Federal/Defense Subcontractors (5–50 FTE)  
**Strict Compliance Rule:** Zero messages sent. No contact data fabricated. All evidence drawn from public federal procurement records (SAM.gov, GSA Schedules, USASpending.gov) and public corporate domains.

---

## 1. SELECTION & PRIORITIZATION METHODOLOGY

The 10 prospects below were selected from the verified directory of 30 qualified US companies (`commercial/11_qualified_prospects_us_icp.md`) based on 5 objective criteria:

1. **High RFP Exposure:** Companies actively bidding on federal task orders, GSA schedules, or prime contractor subcontracts where solicitations routinely exceed 50–150 pages.
2. **Lean Capture Capacity (5–50 FTE):** Organizations where senior engineers, directors of capture, or founders themselves review solicitations and build compliance matrices manually, rather than maintaining a 10-person dedicated proposal shop.
3. **Domain Alignment:** Direct focus on Managed IT Services (MSP), Managed Security Services (MSSP), CMMC/DFARS cybersecurity compliance, or Defense Systems Engineering.
4. **Verifiable Public Evidence:** Explicit past performance records, GSA schedule contract numbers, CAGE codes, or SAM.gov active registrations.
5. **Legitimate Public Outreach Channels:** Existence of official teaming/subcontracting email addresses, public partner inquiry forms, or public executive LinkedIn presences.
