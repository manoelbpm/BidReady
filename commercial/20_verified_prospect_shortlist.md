## 1. REVISION SUMMARY & METHODOLOGICAL CRITERIA

The previous list (`commercial/12_first_10_prospects.md`) contained inaccurate headcount estimates (e.g., classifying companies with 200–400+ employees as "small businesses of ~40 FTE"). Large defense contractors maintain dedicated proposal shops (often 5 to 15 full-time capture managers using enterprise tools like Deltek GovWin and Loopio) and are **outside the self-service ICP of BidReady ($29–$149/mo)**.

### The Strict ICP Filters Applied:
1. **Core Headcount (Priority 1):** 5 to 50 employees. Lean teams where founders, senior architects, or capture directors directly write proposals and build compliance matrices.
2. **Secondary Headcount (Priority 2):** 51 to 100 employees, strictly where evidence indicates agile operations and high task-order bidding volume without bloated proposal departments.
3. **Documented Procurement Activity:** Active GSA MAS Schedule, GWAC (STARS III, Polaris, OASIS+), or verified federal prime/subcontracts.
4. **Demonstrated RFP Pain:** High volume of technical solicitations requiring detailed compliance cross-referencing against internal capabilities.
5. **Public & Legitimate Contact:** Official teaming inboxes, verified corporate partner web forms, or confirmed executive LinkedIn presences.
