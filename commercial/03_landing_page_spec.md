## 7. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ & OBJECTIONS)

### Q: Will my proprietary past performance data or RFP documents be used to train AI models?
**A:** **Never.** BidReady enforces strict multi-tenant database isolation (Supabase Row-Level Security). Our AI processing pipeline uses zero-data-retention enterprise APIs. Your proprietary documents and bid evidence belong strictly to your organization.

### Q: Does BidReady guarantee that our proposal will win the contract?
**A:** **No software can honestly guarantee a contract win.** Government and commercial awards depend on price reasonableness, past performance CPAR ratings, and evaluator discretion. BidReady's goal is to guarantee you **never get disqualified for administrative oversight** and to cut your compliance matrix prep time by 80%.

### Q: Can BidReady handle scanned PDFs and complex government formatting?
**A:** BidReady natively parses digital text from PDF, DOCX, and XLSX files, including complex multi-column tables, amendment attachments, and structured federal schedules. However, BidReady does not perform OCR on scanned image-only PDFs. If a scanned PDF lacking an extractable text layer is uploaded, the system automatically detects it, displays an alert, and releases your quota without consuming analysis credits, prompting you to provide a searchable PDF or Word document.

### Q: How does BidReady differ from running ChatGPT or Claude?
**A:** General LLMs cannot maintain a structured, persistent evidence bank of your company's case studies, they lack deterministic page citations, and they frequently hallucinate or drop clauses when processing 100+ page documents. BidReady is purpose-built with citation provenance and audit trails for procurement professionals.

### Q: Can I cancel my subscription if our bidding cycle slows down?
**A:** Yes, anytime with one click in your billing settings. There are no annual lock-ins, cancellation penalties, or sales calls required.
