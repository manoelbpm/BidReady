## 4. HANDLING COMMON OBJECTIONS

### Objection 1: Security, CUI & Data Privacy (*"We handle CUI / defense solicitations; is BidReady certified for CUI and does AI train on our data?"*)
> **Response:**
> *"That is an essential question for any defense contractor. To be completely transparent:
> 1. **CUI & Regulatory Scope:** BidReady is NOT certified, authorized, or designed for processing Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI), ITAR-controlled technical data, or classified national security documents. It is built strictly for public federal solicitations, public SAM.gov RFPs, GSA task order packages, and commercial bids.
> 2. **Zero Public Model Training:** Uploaded solicitations and capability statements are processed strictly via private enterprise inference APIs. Your documents and data are never used to train or fine-tune public AI models.
> 3. **Tenant Isolation:** All extracted clauses, requirements, and matrices are partitioned at the database level with strict row-level security (RLS).
> 4. **Retention Control:** You retain full ownership of your data and can permanently delete documents and extracted matrices from your workspace at any time."*

### Objection 2: Accuracy, AI Hallucination & Compliance Proof (*"How do we know the AI won't miss a clause, and does semantic matching prove we are compliant?"*)
> **Response:**
> *"BidReady is an assistive intelligence tool designed around human verification, not an autonomous proposal bot:
> 1. **Deterministic Citations:** Every extracted requirement cites the exact page, section, and verbatim sentence from the source PDF.
> 2. **Assistance, Not Legal Determination:** BidReady assists document decomposition and triage, but does not provide legal advice or guarantee regulatory compliance. Human review by your capture team is always required.
> 3. **Semantic Similarity is Not Proof of Compliance:** Semantic matching highlights potential matching evidence from your past performance for human evaluation—it does not constitute certified proof of contractual satisfaction.
> 4. **Blind Testing:** We encourage every team to upload a past RFP they already evaluated manually to directly verify the extraction accuracy against their own verified compliance matrix."*

### Objection 3: Pricing & Commitment (*"We already pay for GovWin / we don't have budget for another enterprise tool."*)
> **Response:**
> *"BidReady is completely different from enterprise capture databases like GovWin or Deltek:
> - GovWin helps you **find** opportunities months in advance. BidReady helps you **dissect the actual solicitation document** once it drops.
> - There are no \$15,000 annual contracts, onboarding fees, or sales reps. BidReady is month-to-month SaaS starting at \$29/month (Starter: 10 analyses) up to \$69/month (Pro: 40 analyses), and you can cancel anytime directly from your customer portal."*
